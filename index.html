StockPulse Pro — User Guide

Market intelligence tool powered by cloud scraping technology

1. Anti-Bot Cloud Engine

This extractor bypasses Adobe Stock's bot mitigation and retrieves verified marketplace data using Apify Proxy.

2. How to connect your Apify API

  1. Visit Apify Console → Integrations → API Tokens.
  2. Copy your token (format: apify_api_...).
  3. Click Connect Apify Token at the top and paste it, then Save & Apply.

3. Scoring & Market Opportunity

  • Opportunity Score (0–100): measures whether a keyword has high buyer demand relative to saturation, via logarithmic download velocity + consistency of high performers.
  • AI Saturation: percentage of top results generated with Generative AI tools vs traditional work.
  • Trending Ratio: share of assets beating the median download benchmark.
